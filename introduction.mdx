eLoop is a material intelligence platform that combines edge AI with cloud software to transform how e-waste is sorted, valued, and traded. If you run a recycling center, eLoop replaces manual guesswork on the sorting line with real-time computer vision and automated batch valuation. If you operate a refinery, eLoop gives you a verified digital showroom where you can browse, filter, and book high-quality feedstock batches — no cold calls, no spreadsheets.

## The E-Waste Challenge

The global shift to clean energy has driven unprecedented demand for critical minerals like lithium, cobalt, and gold. Yet the e-waste recovery chain that supplies those minerals remains deeply inefficient:

- **No visibility on the sorting line.** Recycling facilities process thousands of devices without diagnostic tools to quantify rare-earth distribution. Workers rely on visual estimation, which is slow, inconsistent, and systematically undervalues material.
- **No organized marketplace for buyers.** Industrial refineries have the metallurgical capacity to extract pure elements from scrap, but lack access to a centralized, verified source of graded feedstock.
- **Fragmented B2B sourcing.** Without a common platform, every transaction between recyclers and refineries involves friction, uncertainty, and significant procurement risk.

## The eLoop Solution

eLoop connects every step of the e-waste supply chain — from physical intake at the recycling yard to a confirmed booking by a refinery buyer — in a single, auditable workflow.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register">
    Log an incoming e-waste shipment in the web app: device type, brand, weight, and source. Every batch starts with a verified intake record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan">
    Connect your Raspberry Pi 5 camera to the sorting line. eLoop's on-device YOLOv8 model classifies components in real time — PCBs, batteries, and more — without sending raw images to the cloud.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Value">
    When you finish a sorting session, eLoop cross-references every classified component against a material composition lookup table and automatically assigns the batch a value tier: **HIGH**, **MEDIUM**, or **LOW**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="List">
    Verified, valued batches are automatically published to the Digital Showroom, where refinery buyers can browse and filter them by tier, material type, and weight.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Book">
    A refinery buyer submits a booking request through the mobile app. eLoop tracks the full procurement lifecycle — from `In Progress` through `Confirmed` or `Cancelled` — in a single state machine.
  </Step>
</Steps>
