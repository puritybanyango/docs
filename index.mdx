eLoop is a material intelligence platform that connects recycling centers and industrial refineries through automated e-waste identification, valuation, and procurement. Using computer vision running directly on your sorting line hardware, eLoop classifies components in real time, assigns value tiers, and surfaces verified batches in a searchable Digital Showroom — all without manual guesswork.

## What eLoop Does

eLoop replaces manual e-waste sorting with a connected, data-driven workflow:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register Device Intake">
    Recycling center operators log incoming e-waste by device type, brand, weight, and source through the web app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan & Classify on the Line">
    A camera-equipped Raspberry Pi 5 captures overhead images of components as they move along the sorting line. An on-device YOLOv8 model classifies each item — PCB or battery — in real time, with no cloud round-trip.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Automatic Batch Valuation">
    Classification results stream to the cloud. eLoop cross-references each component against a material composition lookup table and assigns the batch a value tier: **HIGH**, **MEDIUM**, or **LOW**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="List to the Digital Showroom">
    Once finalized, verified batches are automatically published to the B2B Digital Showroom where refinery buyers can browse, filter by value tier, and submit booking requests.
  </Step>
</Steps>
