eLoop is a two-tenant platform, meaning it serves two distinct types of organizations — **Recycling Centers** and **Refineries** — from the same backend, with each tenant seeing only the features and data relevant to their side of the supply chain. Within each company, access is further controlled by three user roles: **Admin**, **Recycling Center Operator (Worker)**, and **Refinery Operator (Worker)**. Your role determines which screens you can access, which actions you can take, and what data is visible to you.

## The Three Roles

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Recycling Center Operator" icon="recycle">
    **Company type:** Recycling Center

    You register incoming e-waste device intakes, operate the real-time sorting-line scanning dashboard, confirm component classifications as they come off the edge AI, and click **Finish Batch** to close and list a completed batch on the Digital Showroom.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Refinery Operator" icon="flask">
    **Company type:** Refinery

    You browse the Digital Showroom on web or mobile, filter available batches by value tier (HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW), submit booking requests for batches you want to procure, and track the status of your active and historical bookings.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Admin" icon="shield-halved">
    **Company type:** Recycling Center or Refinery

    You have all the permissions of the Worker role for your company type, plus the ability to manage your company profile, add and deactivate users, assign roles, and access platform-wide oversight of your organization's activity.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Role Assignment

The user who creates a company account on eLoop is automatically assigned the **Admin** role for that organization. As Admin, you can add additional Admins and Workers to your team at any time through the **Team Management** section of your company portal.

When you invite a new user, you choose their role during the invitation flow. You can also deactivate a user's access or change their role later without deleting their account history. There is no limit on the number of Admins or Workers you can add to a company.

## Company Types

Your company type is set when your organization is first registered on the platform and determines which features are available to all users in your account.

| Feature | Recycling Center | Refinery |
|---|:---:|:---:|
| Device Intake registration | ✓ | — |
| Sorting-line Scanning Dashboard | ✓ | — |
| Batch Management | ✓ | — |
| Digital Showroom — list batches | ✓ | — |
| Digital Showroom — browse & filter | — | ✓ |
| Batch Booking | — | ✓ |
| Booking history | — | ✓ |
| Team & user management (Admin only) | ✓ | ✓ |
| Company profile management (Admin only) | ✓ | ✓ |

<Tip>
  Admins on both company types can access the eLoop mobile app to manage their team members and view booking activity on the go — you are not limited to the web portal for administrative tasks.
</Tip>
