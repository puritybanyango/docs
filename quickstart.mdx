This guide walks you through everything you need to do to go live with eLoop. By the end, you will have a company account set up, your team members added, and — if you run a recycling center — your first device intake logged and a batch processed through scanning and valuation. Refinery buyers can follow Steps 1–3 and then head straight to the Digital Showroom.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up & Create Your Company">
    Open the eLoop web app and click **Sign Up**. When prompted, choose your organization type — **Recycling Center** or **Refinery**. Then fill in your company details:

    - **Company Name** — your business entity name
    - **Company Type** — selected from the drop-down (Recycling Center or Refinery)
    - **Company Email** — the primary contact address for your organization

    The company email becomes the unique identifier used to verify your Admin account at login, so use an address you control. The user who completes this step is automatically designated as the **Company Admin**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Up Your Admin Account">
    After your company profile is created, eLoop prompts you to set up your personal Admin account. Enter your first name, last name, email address, and a secure password (confirmed twice). Your Admin account is tied to the company email you registered in Step 1 — the system uses it to verify your elevated access level each time you sign in.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add Team Members">
    As Admin, navigate to the **Workers** section of your dashboard and click **Add Worker**. For each team member, enter their first name, last name, and email address, then assign them a role:

    - **Admin** — full access to company settings, worker management, and all operational features
    - **Worker** — operational access scoped to your company type (sorting line for recycling centers, showroom browsing for refineries)

    Repeat this step for every person on your team. You can update roles or deactivate accounts at any time from the Workers section.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Register Your First Device Intake (Recycling Centers only)">
    On your dashboard, open **Device Intake** and click **New Intake**. Log the details of your incoming e-waste shipment:

    - **Device Type** — e.g., laptop, mobile phone, circuit board
    - **Brand** — the manufacturer of the device
    **Weight** — the total weight of the incoming batch
    - **Source** — where the devices came from

    Every batch starts with a verified intake record. This information is carried through the scanning and valuation steps and appears in the final Digital Showroom listing.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Run a Scan & Close a Batch">
    Make sure your Raspberry Pi 5 camera is connected and pointed at the sorting line. From the dashboard, start a new sorting session. eLoop streams the live camera feed and the on-device YOLOv8 model classifies components — PCBs, batteries, and more — automatically as they pass through frame.

    Review the classifications in real time and confirm any items as needed. When sorting is complete, click **Finish Batch**. eLoop immediately:

    1. Calculates material composition across all classified components
    2. Assigns a value tier — **HIGH**, **MEDIUM**, or **LOW**
    3. Publishes the verified batch to the Digital Showroom for refinery buyers to discover
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you are a Refinery buyer, you can skip Steps 4 and 5. Head to the [Digital Showroom guide](/guides/digital-showroom) to learn how to browse verified batch listings and submit booking requests from the mobile app.
</Tip>
