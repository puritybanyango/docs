The eLoop mobile app gives refinery operators and company admins access to the Digital Showroom, booking management, and the worker roster — all optimized for on-the-go use on a phone. Whether you are on the floor of your refinery, in a procurement meeting, or traveling between sites, the mobile app keeps your eLoop workflow within reach.

<Note>
  The mobile app does not support sorting-line scanning. That workflow — connecting to the Raspberry Pi 5 camera station and classifying components in real time — runs through the eLoop **web app** on a device physically present at the sorting line. See the [Scanning & Sorting guide](/guides/scanning-sorting) for details.
</Note>

## Who Uses the Mobile App

The mobile app serves two roles:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Refinery Operator" icon="magnifying-glass">
    Browse the batch catalog in the Digital Showroom, submit booking requests, and view the full history of your bookings with current status badges.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Company Admin" icon="users">
    Everything a Refinery Operator can do, plus full worker management: add new team members, update existing profiles, deactivate accounts, and reset credentials.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Both Recycling Center Admins and Refinery Admins can use the mobile app for team management. The catalog and booking features are scoped to Refinery accounts.

## Signing In

When you open the eLoop mobile app for the first time, you will land on the sign-in screen after a brief splash screen.

1. Enter your **Email** and **Password** — the same credentials you use on the web app.
2. Tap **Sign In**.
3. Your session remains active until you explicitly log out. The app securely stores your session so you do not need to sign in again each time you reopen it.

To end your session, open your profile settings and tap **Log Out**.

## Browsing the Catalog

The **Catalog** tab is your entry point to the Digital Showroom on mobile. It displays all currently Listed batches from recycling centers on the platform.

From the catalog you can:

- **Scroll and browse** the full list of available batches
- **Filter by value tier** — select HIGH, MEDIUM, or LOW to narrow the list
- **Tap any batch** to open its detail view, including the component breakdown and material composition summary

<Tip>
  For a full walkthrough of the booking workflow — including what to check before committing to a batch and how to track your bookings — see the [Digital Showroom guide](/guides/digital-showroom).
</Tip>

## Managing Bookings

The **My Bookings** tab shows all bookings you have submitted, both active and historical. Each booking displays a status badge so you can track where things stand at a glance:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **In Progress** | Your booking request has been submitted and is awaiting confirmation from the recycling center. |
| **Confirmed** | The recycling center has confirmed your booking. The batch is reserved for you. |
| **Cancelled** | The booking was cancelled — either by you or by the recycling center. |

Tap any booking to open the full detail view, including the source recycling center's information, the batch material report, and timestamps for each status change.

## Worker Management (Admins Only)

If your account has the **Admin** role, you will see a **Workers** tab in the bottom navigation bar. From here you can manage your company's entire team roster:

- **View all workers** — see each team member's name, email, and role at a glance.
- **Add a new worker** — tap the add button, enter the new user's first name, last name, email, and assign a role (Admin or Worker).
- **Update a profile** — tap any worker to edit their name, email, or role.
- **Deactivate an account** — suspend a team member's access without permanently deleting their records. This is the recommended approach when someone leaves the company.

All changes made in the Workers tab on mobile are reflected immediately across the platform, including the web app.
