The eLoop Digital Showroom is the B2B marketplace where refinery buyers discover, filter, and book verified e-waste batches. Every batch in the Showroom has been AI-classified on-site at the source recycling facility and assigned a value tier by eLoop, giving you verified material intelligence — component counts, metal composition estimates, and a clear tier grade — before you commit to a purchase.

## Accessing the Showroom

The Digital Showroom is available on both the **eLoop web app** and the **eLoop mobile app**. If you are a Refinery operator, the Showroom catalog is the first thing you see after signing in — no additional navigation required.

Recycling Center operators can also view their own Listed batches through the Catalogue section of their dashboard, but the full buyer-facing browsing and booking experience is designed for Refinery accounts.

## Browsing Batches

The Showroom catalog displays all currently Listed batches from recycling centers on the platform. Each batch card in the list shows:

- **Value tier badge** — HIGH, MEDIUM, or LOW at a glance
- **Component summary** — total count of PCBs and Batteries in the batch
- **Source recycling center** — the facility that sorted and listed the batch
- **Listing date** — when the batch was published to the Showroom

Use the **value tier filter** to narrow the catalog to batches that match your procurement targets.

<Tip>
  Filter by **HIGH** tier first to surface the batches with the strongest concentration of precious and critical metals — these represent the highest-value feedstock available on the platform.
</Tip>

## Viewing Batch Details

Click any batch in the catalog to open its full detail view. The detail page shows:

- **Full component breakdown** — the exact count of each classified component type
- **Material composition report** — estimated percentage yields for recoverable metals including Copper, Gold, Silver, Lithium, and others, visualized as a chart
- **Batch metadata** — Batch ID, total weight, value tier, and listing date
- **Source facility** — the recycling center that sorted the batch

You can also download the full material report from this page for use in your procurement records or internal pricing models.

## Booking a Batch

<Steps>

  <Step title="Open the Batch Detail Page">
    From the Showroom catalog, click the batch you want to book to open its full detail view. Review the component breakdown and material composition report to confirm the batch meets your requirements.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Book This Batch">
    Click the **Book This Batch** button at the bottom of the detail page. This is only available on batches with **Listed** status — if the button is greyed out or shows a locked state, the batch has already been booked by another buyer.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm Your Booking Request">
    A confirmation dialog will appear summarising the batch details. Review and click **Confirm** to submit your booking request.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Booking Created — In Progress">
    Your booking is created with status **In Progress** while the recycling center reviews and acknowledges it on their end. You will see the booking appear in your **Bookings** section immediately.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Booking Confirmed">
    Once the recycling center confirms the booking, the batch status transitions to **Booked** and your booking record updates to **Confirmed**. At this point, the batch is reserved exclusively for you and removed from the available catalog.
  </Step>

</Steps>

<Note>
  Each batch can only be booked by one buyer at a time. If a batch you were interested in now shows status **Booked**, it is no longer available and cannot be reserved. Return to the catalog to find the next best option for your sourcing needs.
</Note>

## Tracking Your Bookings

All your bookings — active and historical — are available in the **Bookings** section of the app. Each booking shows:

- **Booking ID** and the associated batch details
- **Current status** — In Progress, Confirmed, or Cancelled
- **Booking date** and last updated timestamp

Click any booking to view the full details including the source recycling center's information and the batch material report. Use this view to coordinate logistics and confirm receipt once the physical material arrives at your facility.
