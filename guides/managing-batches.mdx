Once a sorting scan is finalized, eLoop creates a Batch record containing the scan results, an automated valuation tier, and a lifecycle status. This guide covers how to view, manage, and progress your batches from the moment a scan closes through to a completed transaction with a refinery buyer.

For a deeper conceptual explanation of how batches move through the platform, see the [Batch Lifecycle](/concepts/batch-lifecycle) page.

## Batch Status Overview

Every batch moves through a defined set of statuses as it progresses from your sorting line to a completed sale:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **In Progress** | The scan session is open and components are still being classified. |
| **Listed** | The scan is closed, valuation is assigned, and the batch is visible in the Digital Showroom. |
| **Booked** | A refinery buyer has submitted and confirmed a booking for this batch. |
| **Completed** | The physical transaction has taken place and the batch is closed out. |

## Viewing Your Batches

Open the **Batches** section from the eLoop dashboard sidebar to see your full batch history. Batches are displayed in a paginated table sorted by date, with columns for Batch ID, Creator, Date, Status, Tier, and Category.

Click any row to open the individual batch detail view. From there you can see:

- **Valuation tier** — HIGH, MEDIUM, or LOW, assigned automatically based on the component mix
- **Component breakdown** — the count of PCBs, Batteries, and other classified items in this batch
- **Material composition** — percentage sliders showing estimated recoverable metals (Copper, Gold, Silver, Lithium, and others)
- **Current status** — where the batch sits in the lifecycle right now

## Finalizing and Listing a Batch

Clicking **Finish Batch** at the end of a scan session triggers a sequence of automated steps:

<Steps>

  <Step title="Scan Session Closes">
    The camera feed and classification stream are stopped. No further components can be added to this batch.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Automated Valuation Runs">
    eLoop cross-references every classified component in the session against the material composition lookup table to calculate estimated metal yields for the batch as a whole.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Value Tier is Assigned">
    Based on the composition calculation, the batch receives one of three overall value tiers:

    - **HIGH** — strong concentration of precious or critical metals
    - **MEDIUM** — moderate recoverable value
    - **LOW** — lower-grade material mix
  </Step>

  <Step title="Status Transitions to Listed">
    The batch status moves from **In Progress** to **Listed** automatically. You do not need to take any manual action to publish it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Batch Appears in the Digital Showroom">
    The Listed batch is immediately visible to refinery buyers browsing the Digital Showroom catalog. Buyers can see the value tier badge, component summary, and your facility as the source.
  </Step>

</Steps>

## Managing Active Bookings

Once a refinery buyer books your batch, its status transitions to **Booked**. At this point:

- Open the batch detail view to see the associated booking information, including the buyer's details and the booking timestamp.
- The batch is locked — it no longer appears as available to other buyers in the Showroom.
- When the physical handover or transaction is complete, open the batch and update its status to **Completed** to close it out in your records.

<Warning>
  Marking a batch as **Completed** is irreversible. Once a batch is completed, it cannot be re-listed or re-opened for booking. Make sure the transaction is fully resolved before updating the status.
</Warning>

## Deleting a Batch

If you created a batch by mistake — for example, you opened a scan session accidentally and closed it immediately — you can delete it from the batch detail view.

**Deletion rules:**

- Batches can only be deleted while their status is **In Progress**.
- Once a batch has been Listed or Booked, it cannot be deleted.
- Deletion is permanent and removes all associated scan records for that session.

If you need to remove a Listed or Booked batch, contact your eLoop account administrator for assistance.
