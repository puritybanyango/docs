This guide walks you through creating your company account on eLoop, setting up the first Admin profile, and adding your team members so your whole operation is ready to go from day one.

<Steps>

  <Step title="Navigate to Sign Up">
    Open the eLoop web app. On the welcome screen, click **Sign Up** to begin the registration wizard. If you already have an account, click **Sign In** instead.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose Your Organization Type">
    Select either **Recycling Center** or **Refinery** as your organization type. This choice determines which features and dashboards are available to your company — recycling centers get the sorting-line scanning tools, while refineries access the Digital Showroom for batch procurement.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create Your Company Profile">
    Fill in your company's core details:

    | Field | Description |
    |---|---|
    | **Company Name** | The legal or trading name of your business |
    | **Company Type** | Pre-filled from your selection in the previous step |
    | **Company Email** | The primary inbox for your organization |

    <Note>
      Your **Company Email** is the unique identifier that eLoop uses to verify your Admin account at login. Use a dedicated company inbox — such as `ops@yourcompany.com` — rather than a personal email address. This keeps access secure if staff changes occur.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create Your Admin Account">
    Set up the individual profile for the person registering the company. Enter your:

    - **First Name** and **Last Name**
    - **Email** address (this becomes your personal login credential)
    - **Password** and **Confirm Password**

    The user who completes this step is automatically assigned the **Admin** role for the company. Admins have full access to team management and all platform features available to their company type.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add Team Members">
    Once you are logged in, navigate to the **Workers** section from the main dashboard. Click **Add Worker** and fill in the new user's details:

    - **First Name** and **Last Name**
    - **Email** (this becomes their login credential)
    - **Role** — choose **Admin** for full management access, or **Worker** for operational access

    Repeat this for every team member you want to onboard.

    <Tip>
      Add at least one additional Admin as a backup. If the primary Admin is unavailable, a second Admin can manage the team, reset passwords, and keep operations running without interruption.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

</Steps>

## Password Reset

If you forget your password, click **Forgot Password** on the sign-in screen. Enter the email address associated with your account and eLoop will send you a password reset link.

When you open the reset link, you will be prompted to enter:

- **New Password**
- **Confirm New Password**

Submit the form to update your credentials immediately. The reset applies only to your individual account — your company profile and team roster are not affected.

## Signing In

To sign in to the eLoop web app:

1. Go to the eLoop web app and click **Sign In** on the welcome screen.
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**.
3. Your session token is stored for the duration of your browser session, so you stay logged in as you navigate between pages.
4. When you are done, click **Log Out** to clear your session and return to the welcome screen. This is especially important on shared workstations.
