eLoop is designed with security at every layer — from the edge camera on your sorting line to the cloud database that stores your batch and procurement data. This page explains the protections in place so you can understand exactly how your organization's information is handled.

## Data in Transit

All communication between your devices, the eLoop web app, and the eLoop cloud is encrypted using HTTPS with TLS 1.3. Plain HTTP requests are blocked entirely and will not be accepted by the platform.

This applies across every surface that connects to eLoop:

<Note>
  All production traffic is routed through the Heroku-managed TLS layer. You do not need to configure any client-side certificates or additional TLS settings — encryption is handled automatically for all eLoop connections.
</Note>

## Edge Privacy

The Raspberry Pi camera unit on your sorting line classifies e-waste components locally, on the device itself. Only the lightweight classification result — a component type label such as "PCB" or "battery" — is sent to the eLoop cloud. Raw video and image data are processed entirely on-site and are never transmitted to or stored in the cloud.

This design means sensitive footage of your facility stays within your facility. You get the AI classification intelligence without exposing your operations to unnecessary data transmission.

## Authentication Security

Every API request to eLoop is authenticated independently using a JWT (JSON Web Token). Tokens are stateless — there is no centralized server session to compromise. Each token is scoped to the authenticated user's specific role and company, so a token belonging to one user cannot be used to access another user's data or perform actions outside that user's role.

Sign in is protected by rate limiting and account lockout policies to guard against automated credential-guessing attacks. After a defined number of failed attempts, accounts are temporarily locked as an additional safeguard.

## Multi-Tenant Isolation

eLoop is a multi-tenant platform serving recycling centers and refineries. Each company's data — including batch records, device intakes, bookings, and user accounts — is isolated under that company's account. Users authenticated under your organization can only access data that belongs to your organization.

There is no cross-tenant data visibility. A worker at one recycling center cannot view or interact with the records of another company, even if both are using the eLoop platform simultaneously.

## Role-Based Access Control

Access within your organization is governed by three roles. Admins assign roles when provisioning accounts, and role permissions are enforced on every API request.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Admin" icon="shield-check">
    Full access to the company account — manages users, views all batches, and controls account settings. Admins can deactivate any user account to immediately revoke their access.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Recycling Center Worker" icon="recycle">
    Operates the sorting line, submits batch and device intake records, and views their own organization's processing history.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Refinery Operator" icon="industry">
    Browses available material batches in the digital showroom and places bookings for procurement. Cannot access recycling center operational data.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

If an employee leaves your organization or changes roles, ask your Admin to deactivate or update their account immediately. Deactivating an account invalidates all active tokens for that user at the next request.

<Tip>
  Register your password recovery email to a company-controlled inbox rather than a personal email address. This ensures that if an employee leaves, your organization retains control of the account recovery process and is not locked out of critical admin access.
</Tip>
